# SkillMirror AI

AI-powered mock interview coach that analyzes speech, delivery, and visible behavioral signals to help users improve their interview performance.

## 🎯 Problem

Public speaking anxiety and interview nerves can cause filler words, rushed speech, weak eye contact, and nervous body language.

## 💡 Solution

SkillMirror AI conducts mock interviews and analyzes:

- Filler words
- Speaking pace
- Pauses
- Speech patterns
- Eye contact
- Head movement
- Body language

It then generates an actionable AI coaching report.
